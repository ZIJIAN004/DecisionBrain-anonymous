# Original Formulation: Multiple-Depot Integrated Vehicle and Crew Scheduling Problem (MD-VCSP)

*Source: Multiple-Depot Integrated Vehicle and Crew Scheduling, Dennis Huisman, Richard Freling, Albert P. M. Wagelmans, 2005 (Transportation Science 39(4), pp. 491–502).*

The canonical formulation studied by the paper is **MD-VCSP1**, introduced in §2 (“Mathematical Formulation”). Its notation ($y^d_{ij}$, $x^d_k$) is carried into the algorithm of §3 and the computational experiments of §5. The reformulation MD-VCSP2 of §4 (“Alternative Approach”), obtained by substituting out the $y$ variables, is recorded under *Variants*.

## Sets and Indices

- $N=\{1,2,\dots,n\}$: set of trips, numbered by increasing starting time.

- $E=\{(i,j)\mid i<j,\ i\text{ and }j\text{ compatible},\ i\in N,\ j\in N\}$: set of deadheads.

- $D$: set of depots.

- $s^d,\ t^d$: source and sink nodes representing depot $d$.

- $G^d=(V^d,A^d)$: acyclic directed vehicle-scheduling network for depot $d$, with $V^d=N^d\cup\{s^d,t^d\}$ and $A^d=E^d\cup(s^d\times N^d)\cup(N^d\times t^d)$, where $N^d\subseteq N$ and $E^d\subseteq E$ are the trips and deadheads corresponding to depot $d$.

- $A^{sd}\subset A^d$: set of *short* arcs (idle time between two consecutive trips is too short for the vehicle to return to the depot).

- $A^{ld}\subset A^d$: set of *long* arcs (idle time is long enough for the vehicle to return to the depot in between).

- $K^d$: set of feasible duties corresponding to depot $d$ (exponentially large; generated by column generation).

- $K^d(i)$: set of duties covering the trip task of trip $i\in N^d$ (each trip corresponds to exactly one trip task).

- $K^d(i,j)$: set of duties covering the dh-task of short arc $(i,j)\in A^{sd}$.

- $K^d(i,t^d)$: set of duties covering the dh-task from $el_i$ (end location of trip $i$) to depot $d$.

- $K^d(s^d,j)$: set of duties covering the dh-task from depot $d$ to $bl_j$ (begin location of trip $j$).

- $el_i$, $bl_j$: ending location of trip $i$, beginning location of trip $j$.

## Parameters

- $c^d_{ij}$: vehicle cost of arc $(i,j)\in A^d$ (usually a function of travel and idle time; a fixed vehicle cost may be added to arcs $(s^d,i)$ or $(j,t^d)$).

- $f^d_k$: crew cost of duty $k\in K^d$ (usually a combination of fixed costs such as wages and variable costs such as overtime payment).

## Decision Variables

- $y^d_{ij}\in\{0,1\}$: $1$ if arc $(i,j)$ is used and assigned to depot $d$, $0$ otherwise, for all $d\in D$, $(i,j)\in A^d$.

- $x^d_k\in\{0,1\}$: $1$ if duty $k$ corresponding to depot $d$ is selected, $0$ otherwise, for all $d\in D$, $k\in K^d$.

## Objective

$$\begin{align}
\min\ & \sum_{d\in D}\sum_{(i,j)\in A^d} c^d_{ij}\,y^d_{ij}
      + \sum_{d\in D}\sum_{k\in K^d} f^d_k\,x^d_k \tag{1}
\end{align}$$

## Constraints

$$\begin{align}
\sum_{d\in D}\ \sum_{j:(i,j)\in A^d} y^d_{ij} &= 1, & \forall i\in N \tag{2}\\[2pt]
\sum_{d\in D}\ \sum_{i:(i,j)\in A^d} y^d_{ij} &= 1, & \forall j\in N \tag{3}\\[2pt]
\sum_{i:(i,j)\in A^d} y^d_{ij} - \sum_{i:(j,i)\in A^d} y^d_{ji} &= 0, & \forall d\in D,\ \forall j\in N^d \tag{4}\\[2pt]
\sum_{k\in K^d(i)} x^d_k - \sum_{j:(i,j)\in A^d} y^d_{ij} &= 0, & \forall d\in D,\ \forall i\in N^d \tag{5}\\[2pt]
\sum_{k\in K^d(i,j)} x^d_k - y^d_{ij} &= 0, & \forall d\in D,\ \forall (i,j)\in A^{sd} \tag{6}\\[2pt]
\sum_{k\in K^d(i,t^d)} x^d_k - y^d_{i\,t^d} - \sum_{j:(i,j)\in A^{ld}} y^d_{ij} &= 0, & \forall d\in D,\ \forall i\in N^d \tag{7}\\[2pt]
\sum_{k\in K^d(s^d,j)} x^d_k - y^d_{s^d\,j} - \sum_{i:(i,j)\in A^{ld}} y^d_{ij} &= 0, & \forall d\in D,\ \forall j\in N^d \tag{8}\\[2pt]
x^d_k,\ y^d_{ij} &\in \{0,1\}, & \forall d\in D,\ \forall k\in K^d,\ \forall (i,j)\in A^d \tag{9}
\end{align}$$
